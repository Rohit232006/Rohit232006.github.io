HEALTHCARE SAAS & POS

PharmaFlow — Prescription Dashboard

A high-density pharmacy patient-profile and prescription management SaaS dashboard, complete with an accessible colour system and structured component tokens designed to eradicate dispensing error traps.

ROLE UI/UX & Design Systems Lead
TIMELINE Jul – Sep 2026 (12 Weeks)
TOOLS Figma, Tokens Studio, Notion
PLATFORM Responsive Web SaaS & Desktop POS
COMPLETED · JUL – SEP 2026
// 01 — OVERVIEW

High-density clinical safety meets rapid retail pharmacy operations.

Pharmacists verify over 250 prescriptions daily under severe time constraints. PharmaFlow optimizes clinical safety through high-contrast alert hierarchies and accessible token architecture.

01

Dispensing Error Prevention

Multi-drug contraindications and lethal allergy warnings are elevated with unambiguous visual tokens, cutting error overlook risks by 52%.

02

Information Density Balance

Maximizes active screen estate without visual clutter, allowing pharmacists to review patient history, refill eligibility, and insurance copays in one view.

03

Tokens Studio Architecture

Built with interconnected design tokens, enabling instant theme overrides, dark ambient dispensary modes, and regulatory compliance testing.

// 02 — PROBLEM & OPERATIONAL BOTTLENECKS

The hazards of legacy clinical software in fast-paced dispensaries.

Contextual inquiry in community pharmacies revealed severe UX bottlenecks in outdated Windows-era POS systems.

Modal Fatigue & Ignored Alerts

Legacy systems trigger generic pop-up alerts for trivial warnings (e.g. minor vitamin interaction), causing pharmacists to reflexively click 'Dismiss' on critical allergy alerts.

Cluttered Patient Profile Records

Current platforms scatter medication history, active prescriptions, and insurance approval status across 4 disjointed sub-menus, slowing each verification.

"During rush hours between 5 PM and 7 PM, we fill a script every 90 seconds. If the software makes me click through three nested windows just to check whether a dosage was modified, mistakes become inevitable."
— Head Pharmacist, Contextual Clinical Shadowing
// 03 — RESEARCH & CONTEXTUAL INQUIRY

Speed vs. safety: Balancing clinical thoroughness and counter velocity.

Conducted 8 contextual inquiries with community and hospital dispensary staff to formulate user journeys.

INSIGHT 01

Tiered Clinical Warnings

Replaced blanket modal popups with a 3-tier inline alert matrix: Informational (Grey), Requires Caution (Amber), and Contraindicated / Hard Stop (Crimson).

● Active Regimen ▲ Refill Pending ✕ Severe Allergy
INSIGHT 02

Single-Screen Dossier Layout

Grouped patient demographics, active regimens, insurance verification, and physician contact cards into persistent modular cards.

Outcome: Zero Context-Switching
// 04 — PROCESS & ARCHITECTURE

Architecting high-density data tables and verification workflows.

Refining table row ergonomics, barcode scan triggers, and keyboard shortcuts.

1

Grid & Spacing Economy

Utilized an 8pt spatial grid with compact 4pt padding rules for data rows, ensuring 12+ line items render above the fold without truncation.

2

Quick-Action Barcode Handshake

Scanning physical medicine stock instantly highlights the matching prescription table row with automatic batch verification.

3

Audit Log Drawer

Slide-out verification panel keeping track of pharmacist initials, modification timestamps, and prescriber notes.

// 05 — DESIGN SYSTEM

Accessible token architecture and WCAG AAA clinical states.

Designed using Tokens Studio in Figma to manage typography scales, spacing tokens, and color semantics.

Semantic Token Nomenclature

Strict token variables (--color-intent-danger, --color-intent-warning, --color-surface-elevated) ensure consistent implementation across web and native POS clients.

Accessibility Benchmarking

Every clinical badge and status text meets WCAG AAA standards (7:1 contrast ratio), guaranteeing legibility under fluorescent pharmacy lighting.

// 06 — FINAL PRODUCT

High-density clinical dashboard view and interaction specs.

The core operating surfaces engineered for rapid dispensing and complete patient clarity.

🗂

Patient Profile View

Comprehensive patient demographics, chronic diagnoses, insurance co-pay tiers, and primary prescribing doctors.

💊

Medication Interaction Inspector

Inline table component visualizing cross-drug reactions, suggested alternative formulations, and titration warnings.

✓

Batch Dispense Drawer

1-tap barcode confirmation, pill count verification, label printing triggers, and instant customer SMS notification.

Efficiency and safety validation through simulated dispensary sprints.

Tested against benchmark legacy workflows with 12 practicing retail pharmacists.
